\chapter*{Abstract} 
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This thesis presents the development of MindCare-AI, an intelligent mobile application dedicated to mental health monitoring and improvement, developed within Mobelite company. The solution combines physiological signal analysis through remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) and automatic emotion detection from text to assess users' psychological states. The system integrates an adaptive therapeutic chatbot providing personalized real-time support based on artificial intelligence. The methodology relies on an agile approach for development and advanced data science techniques (CRISP-DM) for data analysis. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of this innovative approach for non-invasive and personalized mental health monitoring.
