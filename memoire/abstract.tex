\chapter*{Abstract} 
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This thesis presents the development of MindCare-AI, an innovative solution dedicated to monitoring and improving mental well-being. Conducted within the company Mobelite, this project aims to address major mental health challenges by offering an intelligent, accessible, and personalized mobile application. MindCare-AI combines the analysis of physiological data (using remote photoplethysmography) and text analysis to assess users’ emotional and physiological states.
The system integrates several complementary modules: acquisition and processing of vital signals, automatic emotion recognition from natural language, extraction of relevant features, and classification using artificial intelligence models. An interactive therapeutic chatbot further enhances the user experience by providing real-time support and personalized advice.
The adopted methodology is based on an agile approach for mobile development and advanced data science techniques for data preparation, preprocessing, and analysis, all automated in Jupyter notebooks to ensure reproducibility. The experimental results demonstrate the relevance and effectiveness of the proposed approach for continuous, non-invasive, and personalized mental health monitoring, paving the way for new perspectives in e-health and psychological prevention.
